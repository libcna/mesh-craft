A 3D scene editor that thinks in shapes, not triangles.

MeshCraft lets you build 3D scenes from primitive shapes, boolean operations, extrusions, and keyframe animation — then export straight to glTF/GLB for use in games, viewers, and other 3D tools.

What you can build

Everything is stored as editable, human-readable .mc3.xml — not baked triangle soup — so you can always go back and change your mind.

📦
Primitive Shapes
Box, Sphere, Cylinder, Cone, Plane, Torus, Capsule, Disk, Grid, and Ico-sphere — all editable after placement.
⚗️
CSG Booleans
Union, Difference, and Intersection — cut holes, merge shapes, and carve real geometry.
🔩
Extrude
Sweep a cross-section — rectangle, circle, polygon, or custom — along a line, arc, helix, polyline, or curve.
🎨
PBR Materials
Color, roughness, metallic, and emissive glow, with texture support — the same material model used by modern game engines.
🎞️
Keyframe Animation
A timeline editor for moving, rotating, scaling, and recoloring objects over time.
🚶
Walk Mode
Drop into first-person and walk around your scene before you ever export it.
🧩
Definitions & Instances
Build something once, then place it a hundred times — edit the original and every copy updates.
📤
glTF/GLB Export
One click sends your scene to Blender, Godot, Unity, or any glTF-compatible engine, animations included.
